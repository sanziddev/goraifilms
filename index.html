Who We Are

Bangladesh's Most Acclaimed Production House

Based in Mirpur DOHS, Dhaka, Gorai Films is a premier storytelling powerhouse founded by renowned filmmaker Fakhrul Arefeen Khan. We specialize in creating cinematic experiences that transcend boundaries and touch human lives.

From critically acclaimed feature films like 'Bhuban Majhi' and 'Gondi', to the groundbreaking English-language film 'JK 1971', and the UNESCO-recognized documentary 'The Speech' — our vision is to craft narratives that inspire change, provoke thought, and entertain audiences globally.

Our Journey

Milestones

2008

Founded — Al-Badar Documentary

Gorai Films established by filmmaker Fakhrul Arefeen Khan. Released debut documentary 'Al-Badar', earning national recognition.

2010

The Speech — UNESCO Recognition

Released 'The Speech' documentary on Bangabandhu's 7th March speech. Used as official content for UNESCO Memory of the World Register.

2017

Bhuban Majhi — Feature Film Debut

Released critically acclaimed war drama 'Bhuban Majhi' starring Parambrata Chatterjee & Aparna Ghosh. Theatrical release in Bangladesh & India.

2020

Gondi — Digital Expansion

Released 'Gondi' starring Sabyasachi Chakrabarty & Suborna Mustafa. Launched YouTube channel, embracing digital content creation.

2023

JK 1971 — International Breakthrough

Released 'JK 1971' — first English-language Bangladeshi film about the Liberation War. Based on true story of PIA plane hijacking.

2024

Global Recognition & Growth

Surpassed 50+ awards internationally. Expanded to 35+ creative professionals. Continuing to craft stories that inspire change worldwide.
